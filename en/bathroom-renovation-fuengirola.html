How we worked

Complete renovation in 2.5 weeks while the owners were abroad.

📍 Location: Fuengirola

This bathroom was completely renovated in just 2.5 weeks.

The old bathtub was replaced with a modern walk-in shower. We installed new plumbing, replaced the entire electrical system, fitted new tiles, sanitary ware and bathroom furniture.

The homeowners were outside Spain during the renovation, so our team managed the entire project from start to finish. We sent daily progress updates and photos, ensuring they stayed informed every step of the way.

One of the biggest challenges was that the existing plumbing and electrical systems were badly worn and needed complete replacement. Despite this, the project was completed on schedule, followed by a professional deep clean before handover.

The clients returned to a fully finished bathroom and were delighted with the result.
